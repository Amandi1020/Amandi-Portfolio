# Content Alignment

Report-only notes on how the portfolio supports a Project Management / Business Analysis internship target. No claims below are added to the live site unless they already exist or were supplied by the owner.

## What supports the target roles

- The About section now leads with the PM/BA goal and explains the finance-plus-IT combination.
- CA Corporate Level and AAT give credible grounding in budgets, risk, reporting and value.
- The Harithawarjana Organizing Committee Vice President (Finance) role is directly relevant leadership evidence.
- The IEEE Standards Committee finance role adds a second committee-level finance and standards example.
- The Bank of Ceylon internship supports documentation, customer service and financial-services familiarity.
- Project Management, Agile Project Management and MIS certificates reinforce the target direction.
- EcoTrack, the Task Management System, Salon Nilu and the Pet Adoption System show full-stack delivery experience.
- The new section order puts Experience before Projects and Skills, so recruiters see leadership evidence early.
- Project filters and a featured EcoTrack card make the strongest project easier to find.

## What dilutes the message

- The project mix still leans toward front-end and full-stack development. The PM/BA relevance is not explained on each card.
- Several tools and skill names still need owner confirmation before they can be defended in an interview.
- The UI/UX certificate is less directly relevant than the PM and Agile certificates, although it shows design awareness.
- The GitHub contributor entry and Pull Shark achievement are developer signals; they are useful but should not crowd out PM/BA evidence.
- Percentage skill bars were removed because the figures were not verifiable and did not add useful evidence.
- The `react-login-signup` project link currently returns 404.
- `assets/resume.pdf` is approximately 4.2 MB and is not tailored to PM/BA roles.
- There are no quantified outcomes for the leadership roles, and no testimonials.
- The profile image is currently a GitHub avatar copy rather than a chosen professional photo.

## What is missing

- Quantified results for Experience: funds raised, budget managed, attendance, reporting cadence or time saved.
- Project case studies that show the problem, the candidate's role, requirements, stakeholders, process and outcome.
- Evidence of BA artefacts: BRD/FRD, user stories, process maps, traceability matrix or wireframes.
- Evidence of PM artefacts: project plan, schedule, risk register, budget tracker or status report.
- Project screenshots and demo credentials.
- Real testimonials from supervisors, lecturers or committee leads.
- Confirmed O/L results if the owner wants them shown.
- A working FormSubmit activation test.
- A shorter, role-targeted CV.

## Project case-study template

Use this structure for EcoTrack first, then the strongest remaining projects. Do not invent numbers or client names.

```text
Project:
One-line summary:
Context:
  - Why the project existed
  - Who it was for
  - Team size and my role
Problem:
  - The user or business problem
  - Constraints and assumptions
What I did:
  - Requirements gathering
  - Stakeholders and decisions
  - Process or system design
  - Delivery and testing
Tools and methods:
Evidence:
  - Repository
  - Live demo
  - Screenshots
  - Requirements or design artefacts
Outcome:
  - Verified result or measurable change
  - What I learned
```

## Evidence rules

- Keep every existing experience, education, certification, project and contact fact unchanged unless the owner corrects it.
- Mark unknown details with `TODO(owner)` rather than filling gaps with plausible copy.
- Prefer verified outcomes and artefacts over adjectives.
- Do not publish tool names, metrics, grades or achievements the owner cannot defend in an interview.
